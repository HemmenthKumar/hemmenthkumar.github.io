DRAGON UAV//AWACS

Long-Range Autonomous Search & Rescue Aircraft

The Problem

Current Search and Rescue (SAR) missions rely on helicopters (high operating costs, complex logistics) or standard drones (insufficient flight times of ~90 minutes). SAR requires persistent, long-range aerial surveying that traditional multicopters physically cannot provide due to energy density limits.

The Solution

An autonomous electric glider featuring VTOL capabilities transitioning into Twin Motor Flight Mode (TMFM). Designed for massive endurance, it achieves an 8+ hour flight time. It streams live video, utilizes thermal/night vision, and maps terrain using 3D Gaussian Splatting (OpenSplat) to bypass heavy/expensive Lidar systems.

Manufacturing & Avionics

The fuselage and wings utilize a composite approach: Expanded Polypropylene (EPP) foam core, covered by 100 GSM fiberglass and 50/36 GSM Kevlar, stiffened with carbon fiber spars. Avionics are driven by an ArduPilot Mega 2.8 flight controller and an ODroid XU4 companion computer running OpenHD for beyond-line-of-sight video transmission.

BURST SWARM AWACS

Autonomous Aerial Mesh Node Deployment System

The Problem

During natural disasters, existing cellular networks are immediately compromised. First responders enter "denied environments" where establishing command-and-control communications is slow, dangerous, and physically constrained by line-of-sight.

The Solution

BURST SWARM AWACS is a cylindrical carrier drone (deployer) that ascends and uses controlled centrifugal force to scatter 7 independent, solar-powered communication nodes. The nodes form an instant hexagonal Meshtastic (LoRa) mesh network covering 4 km², restoring connectivity for first responders in under 90 seconds without human footprints.

Deployment Mechanics

The carrier utilizes dual contra-rotating brushless motors for stable hover. Differential ESC control induces a spin, tilting the internal cradle horizontal. Six outer nodes eject radially at 60° intervals, and one central node drops vertically. No parachutes are required due to the nodes' aerodynamic stability and shock-absorbing IP67 casing.

Carrier Specifications

  • Dimensions Ø 25 cm × H 45 cm
  • Empty Weight 1.2 kg
  • Payload 7 Nodes (~800g total)
  • Deployment < 90 Seconds
  • Drop Accuracy ±2 m (GPS + IMU)

Node Specifications

  • Radio SX1262 LoRa + ESP32
  • Power 2000mAh LiFePO₄
  • Solar Panel 6V 2W, Auto-deploy
  • Range (LOS) 5–8 km per node
  • Protocol Meshtastic v2+ (Encrypted)

RESTORATION PROJECT // IN PROGRESS

ELECTRIC VIOLIN

Piezoelectric Stereo Amplification & Acrylic Conversion

The Challenge

A used violin suffered a full belly separation, with the top panel split in half. Traditional restoration was bypassed in favor of a modern engineering solution that would restore functionality while enhancing the instrument's capabilities through electronics.

The Solution (Prototype V1)

The damaged wooden belly panel was replaced by a custom laser-cut acrylic panel. To compensate for the loss of acoustic resonance, a custom piezoelectric amplification system was designed. Ceramic piezoelectric crystals placed on each bridge foot capture stereo sound by converting mechanical strains into electrical current. The circuit is designed with an 11x gain, delivering crisp, clear sound far louder than its acoustic counterpart.

Technical Specifications

  • Body Material Laser-Cut Acrylic
  • Pickup Type Ceramic Piezoelectric
  • Audio Mode Stereo (Bridge Feet)
  • Circuit Gain 11x

Project Timeline

  • PCB Fabrication March 26th
  • Working Prototype April 2nd
  • Delivery April 8th

Expandability (Roadmap)

In practice the electric violin sounds exactly like its acoustic counterpart, but it is also possible to introduce effects, right from adding distortion within the circuit design, to live audio processing through an onboard computer such as a raspberry pi. This would allow for direct software effects through DAWs, and use and creation of endless pedals to the artist’s liking. That would be the ultimate electric violin. The current prototype serves as the platform for these future integrations.

COMPUTATIONAL PHYSICS // BROWSER SIMULATION

BLACKHOLE ACCRETION DISK SIMULATOR

Schwarzschild Geodesic Equations Visualization

The Concept

A real-time gravitational physics simulation where asteroids spawn on mouse click and orbit a central singularity. The simulation calculates trajectories strictly for mass, visualizing the formation of an accretion disk.

The Tech

Built entirely in JavaScript using the p5.js library. The renderer solves the Schwarzschild geodesic equations numerically for every asteroid, demonstrating how stable orbits form and decay near the event horizon. Visuals are inspired by the classic game Asteroids, featuring vector-style graphics and a stark white circle highlighting the event horizon.
