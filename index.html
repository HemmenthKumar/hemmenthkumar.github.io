FILE: BURST_SWARM // AWACS PROOF OF CONCEPT

Aerial Mesh Node Deployment

Autonomous Disaster Response Network

The Problem

During natural disasters, cellular networks fail. First responders enter denied environments where establishing command-and-control communications is slow and physically constrained.

The Solution

A carrier drone that ascends and uses controlled centrifugal force to scatter 7 independent, solar-powered communication nodes. Forms an instant Meshtastic (LoRa) mesh network covering 4 km² in under 90 seconds.

FILE: E-VIOLIN // V1 IN PROGRESS

Piezoelectric Stereo Amplification

Acrylic Conversion & Custom PCB Audio Circuit

The Challenge

A used violin suffered full belly separation. Traditional restoration was bypassed in favor of a modern engineering solution that would enhance the instrument's capabilities through electronics.

The Solution

The wooden belly was replaced by laser-cut acrylic. Ceramic piezoelectric crystals on each bridge foot capture stereo sound. The custom circuit delivers 11x gain, serving as a platform for future DAW integrations and live audio processing via Raspberry Pi.

FILE: SCHWARZSCHILD_SIM LIVE

Blackhole Accretion Disk Simulator

Computational Physics & WebGL Visualization

The Concept

A real-time gravitational physics simulation where asteroids spawn on mouse click and orbit a central singularity. Calculates trajectories strictly for mass, visualizing accretion disk formation.

The Tech

Built in JavaScript using p5.js. Solves Schwarzschild geodesic equations numerically for every asteroid, demonstrating how stable orbits form and decay near the event horizon.
