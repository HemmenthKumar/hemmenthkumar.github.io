RESEARCH MEMORANDUM // 01

Design Philosophy & Rapid Hardware Lifecycles

ESTABLISHED: JUNE 2026 // READ-ONLY

In deep-tech engineering, the chasm between raw mathematical simulation and functional, physically optimized product design is where most hardware endeavors fail. Most designers prioritize the cosmetic shell first, while engineers focus exclusively on structural load equations or circuit routing.

My approach merges these two domains. By evaluating materials, spatial configurations, and end-user parameters alongside structural physics right at the start, I eliminate the typical delay of redesign cycles.

"True rapid prototyping means designing a device's form-factor to adapt to the physical environment rather than forcing the user to conform to technical compromises."

The Science of Structural Fabrication

When designing high-stress hardware like the 14kg DRAGON UAV airframe, I utilize custom multi-layer composite carbon fiber and Kevlar layups. Traditional 3D printed polymers are useful for rapid spatial fits, but fail to handle the structural loads required for extended, high-drag flight operations.

By designing components around CNC carbon panel cuts and structural reinforcement matrices, we transition directly from low-fidelity mockups into space-rated flight components without intermediary redesign stages.

RESEARCH MEMORANDUM // 02

Autonomous Avionics & First-Principles Optimization

ESTABLISHED: JUNE 2026 // READ-ONLY

Modern autonomous aerospace design suffers from excessive system bloat. Developers rely on generalized, high-overhead Linux single-board computers to coordinate basic structural decisions, sacrificing power density and runtime.

Our baseline is built on first-principles reductionism. We strip the onboard communication network to raw, critical telemetry, offloading intensive tasks like high-resolution 3D terrain modeling to passive edge servers via secure, targeted transmissions.

Power Management & Avionics Integration

By managing power distribution networks down to the micro-amp level, we can design efficient flight states. Avionics should operate under strict power-rail isolation. In the DRAGON platform, sensor and radio communication systems operate on isolated power lines to prevent motor-induced signal noise from introducing tracking error into the navigation system.

By routing custom PCB configurations directly to high-capacity Li-ion cell blocks, we avoid thermal throttling and maintain steady structural telemetry, ensuring structural integrity even in intense flight profiles.

RESEARCH MEMORANDUM // 03

Deploying Mesh Infrastructures in Blockaded Regions

ESTABLISHED: JUNE 2026 // READ-ONLY

In disaster recovery or disconnected communication zones, terrestrial networks are often the first infrastructure elements to fail. First responders are routinely forced to work in terrain with zero connectivity, delaying emergency logistics.

The BURST SWARM deployment architecture addresses this challenge. It drops structural, shock-resistant communication nodes directly from an airborne platform to quickly re-establish encrypted local coordination networks.

Decentralized Telemetry Topology

Using standard ESP32 boards paired with high-performance SX1262 LoRa radios, the dropped nodes immediately establish an encrypted mesh layout using the Meshtastic protocol. By using centrifugal dispersion to scatter the units, we guarantee optimal structural distance and signal strength without needing direct manual placement.

This decentralized approach ensures that even if individual nodes are lost or damaged, the surrounding active cells dynamically adjust pathing to maintain communication with the base, providing stable tactical coordination for rescue personnel.
