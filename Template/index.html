Executive Summary

Current Search and Rescue (SAR) missions rely on helicopters (high operating costs, complex logistics) or standard multicopters (insufficient flight times of ~90 minutes). SAR requires persistent, long-range aerial surveying that traditional platforms cannot provide due to energy density limits.

The DRAGON UAV is an autonomous electric glider featuring VTOL capabilities transitioning into Twin Motor Flight Mode (TMFM). Designed for massive endurance, it achieves an 8+ hour flight time. It streams live video, utilizes thermal/night vision, and maps terrain using 3D Gaussian Splatting (OpenSplat) to bypass heavy/expensive Lidar systems.

Manufacturing & Avionics

The airframe utilizes an advanced composite approach: Expanded Polypropylene (EPP) foam core, covered by 100 GSM fiberglass and 50/36 GSM Kevlar, stiffened with carbon fiber spars for maximum strength-to-weight ratio.

How To Build It

Complete build process from raw materials to flight-ready aircraft. Estimated total build time: 6-8 weeks for first prototype.

⚠ Tools & Equipment Required

▸ CNC Hot Wire Foam Cutter
▸ Vacuum Bagging System
▸ Epoxy Resin & Brushes
▸ Carbon Fiber Tubing (6mm-10mm)
▸ Soldering Station (60W+)
▸ 3D Printer (for mounts)
▸ Multimeter & Power Analyzer
▸ Servo Tester & Programming Card
PHASE 1: Airframe Construction Week 1-2
  • Cut EPP foam cores using CNC hot wire cutter (wing & fuselage profiles)
  • Route servo bays, battery compartments, and spar channels
  • Insert carbon fiber spars (main wing + horizontal stabilizer)
  • Apply 100 GSM fiberglass wrap with epoxy resin
  • Apply 50/36 GSM Kevlar reinforcement at high-stress points
  • Vacuum bag and cure for 24 hours
  • Sand, prime, and apply final finish
PHASE 2: Powertrain Installation Week 3
  • Mount AT4120 KV500 motors on motor pods
  • Install 80A HV ESCs with heat sinking
  • Wire power distribution board (PDB) with XT90 connectors
  • Connect battery harness (3x 22.8V packs in parallel)
  • Test motor rotation direction and ESC calibration
  • Verify current draw at static full throttle (target: <80A)
PHASE 3: Avionics Integration Week 4-5
  • Mount ArduPilot APM 2.8 on vibration-damped plate
  • Install ODroid XU4 companion computer with OpenHD
  • Connect GPS M8N module (external mast mount)
  • Wire servos (aileron, elevator, rudder, VTOL flaps)
  • Configure ArduPilot parameters (plane firmware, VTOL mixing)
  • Set up telemetry link (915 MHz SiK radio)
  • Test video transmission range (ground test)
PHASE 4: Ground Testing & Calibration Week 6
  • Verify center of gravity (CG) at 25% MAC
  • Perform control surface throw calibration
  • Run motor test at 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% throttle (30 sec each)
  • Check all servo movements for binding or reverse logic
  • Verify failsafe behavior (RC loss, battery low)
  • Complete pre-flight checklist (30+ items)
PHASE 5: Flight Testing (TRL-7) Week 7-8
  • First flight: Hand launch, 5 min endurance test, manual landing
  • Second flight: VTOL hover test (5 min), transition to forward flight
  • Third flight: Autonomous waypoint mission (2 km loop)
  • Fourth flight: Extended endurance test (target: 2+ hours)
  • Fifth flight: Full mission profile (8+ hour target)
  • Post-flight: Data analysis, log review, iterative tuning
