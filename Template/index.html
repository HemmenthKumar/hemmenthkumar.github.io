Executive Summary

Current Search and Rescue (SAR) missions rely on helicopters (high operating costs, complex logistics) or standard multicopters (insufficient flight times of ~90 minutes). SAR requires persistent, long-range aerial surveying that traditional platforms cannot provide due to energy density limits.

The DRAGON UAV is an autonomous electric glider featuring VTOL capabilities transitioning into Twin Motor Flight Mode (TMFM). Designed for massive endurance, it achieves an 8+ hour flight time. It streams live video, utilizes thermal/night vision, and maps terrain using 3D Gaussian Splatting (OpenSplat) to bypass heavy/expensive Lidar systems.

Manufacturing & Avionics

The airframe utilizes a advanced composite approach: Expanded Polypropylene (EPP) foam core, covered by 100 GSM fiberglass and 50/36 GSM Kevlar, stiffened with carbon fiber spars for maximum strength-to-weight ratio.
